# Energy Update

## What's new

Most of this release follows a look at OpenStrap 2.1 (MIT/Apache-2.0), another open app for
the Helio Strap, and adapts what held up to Ridge.

- **Charge is now Energy, and it no longer freezes when the strap is off.** A stretch without
  the strap drifts down slowly, then shows as a gap in the chart. After a night off the wrist,
  the morning starts from your usual wake value and says it's estimated. A calm, still minute
  now holds Energy rather than slowly filling it. Sitting with a raised heart rate (heat,
  coffee, a tense call) no longer drains it like exercise. The strap's stress reading during
  a workout and for 20 minutes after is ignored. On a night the illness check flags high,
  sleep refills less and the next day drains more.
- **Biological age now comes from your habits.** Resting heart rate, steps, sleep
  regularity, short sleep and strength training, each against the typical person your age
  and sex, over the last six months. It shows as a range in whole years, with a weekly trend
  and what each habit adds or takes off next to its target. It's labelled early until there
  are six months of data. The VO₂max estimated from your profile no longer feeds it.
- **Daily load counts training, not sitting.** Every minute of a workout counts. Outside
  workouts, only minutes you're moving and working hard enough do, so a desk day no longer
  reads as moderate strain. The Strain screen says how the day split between workouts and
  everyday movement. The app works out your whole history again once, on the first sync
  after updating.
- **Each night leads with a headline:** Short night, Broken sleep, Great night, Good night,
  Light sleep or Okay night, with the reason underneath. The strap's sleep score is now
  worded on Zepp's own scale (90 Excellent, 80 Good, 60 Fair).
- **Home-screen widgets.** Today shows Recovery, Energy, last night and steps, with Energy
  through the day at the largest size. Steps shows the count as of the last sync against
  8,000, with the week at the largest size. Each comes in three sizes. When the last sync is
  over three hours old an amber "Synced 09:10" appears; tap it to sync without opening Ridge.
- **Find strap**, on the Strap page, buzzes the strap until you tap Stop, for up to a minute.
- **The Calories tiles** that used to say "Energy" now say Calories, so Energy means one
  thing.
- **Fix:** opening the Strap page during a sync no longer shows "The strap is busy". The
  alarms wait for the sync and then load.

No server change and no database change: just update the app.
