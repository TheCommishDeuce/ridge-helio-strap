# Self-Hosted Update

## What's new

Ridge can now read two other services you may already run yourself. Both are optional and
read-only: Ridge never changes anything in them. Connect them under **Settings →
Integrations**.

- **openGym gym sessions with the strap's heart rate.** Pair Ridge with your
  [openGym](https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym) using the 8-character code from
  openGym's Settings → "Pair the mobile app". Your logged sessions appear on Fitness, and each
  one shows the strap's heart rate through it, its strain, load and heart-rate zones, and your
  exercises and sets by name. When openGym recorded when you ticked each set, each exercise is
  shaded on the heart-rate line and each set is marked, and every set shows the heart rate of
  the minute you did it. A workout the strap detected during a session is shown as that
  session, not twice. Ridge checks openGym for changes after each sync and when you open
  Fitness, so edits and deletions follow.
- **Dawarich walks and rides.** Connect your [Dawarich](https://github.com/Freika/dawarich)
  with its API key, and the day's walks, runs and rides appear on Fitness: each on a map, with
  the distance, the time, and the strap's heart rate, strain and zones through it. "All on one
  map" shows the whole day. Ridge itself still never records your location: the tracks stay
  in Dawarich, are fetched when you look at a day and are not kept on the phone or in the
  backup.
- **The strap decides how you moved, not Dawarich's label.** Dawarich gives a whole track one
  mode and often guesses wrong: a walk with a metro ride in it counts the metro, a walk and a
  tram becomes a bike ride, a bike ride becomes a run. Ridge looks at each stretch between two
  of your tracker's points instead. Running steps are a run, walking steps a walk, a fast
  stretch inside a workout the strap recorded is a ride, and anything faster with no steps
  (a tram, a train, a car) is left out of the distance and drawn dashed on the map.
- **The map is drawn by Ridge** from OpenStreetMap tiles, so the app stays small. Tiles load
  only while a map is on screen, and the tile address is a setting if you run your own tile
  server.
- **Older days open on Fitness.** Tap a past day in the training calendar to see that day's
  workouts, gym sessions, walks and rides; the week strip only reaches back seven days.

No server update needed.
