# Short Walks Update

## What's new

- **A short walk is no longer a workout.** The strap's own detection is sensitive enough to
  file a few minutes on foot as a workout. Ridge now leaves out a strap workout that is under
  10 minutes, easy (an average heart rate under 70 % of your maximum) and walked (steps
  throughout). It is gone from Fitness, Today, the training calendar and the Health Connect
  export, it gets no "Workout detected" notice, and its minutes count as any other minutes of
  the day for your load, strain and calories. Past days are recounted once after the update.
- **Yours to overrule.** For a week Fitness lists each one under its day as "Short walk, not
  a workout" with **Keep**, which makes it a workout again with the strap's own figures.
- **What stays.** A short record without steps is still a workout: it may be a piece of a
  longer session or a short ride. Anything you edited or added is never left out, and the
  strap's records themselves are untouched.
