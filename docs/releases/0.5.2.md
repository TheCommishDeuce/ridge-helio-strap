# Winter Time Change Update

## What's new

- **Sleep keeps its times across a clock change.** The strap records a night by its own
  clock, so on the night the clocks went back everything after 03:00 showed an hour early.
  A night that ended at 07:00 read 06:00, with an hour missing from time in bed. Nights now
  keep the times you slept, in autumn and in spring. Each sync fetches the last two nights
  again, so a night from the last two days is corrected by the first sync after updating. An
  older night stays as it was.
- **The repeated hour counts when you slept through it.** On the night the clocks go back the
  strap scores only one of the two 02:00–03:00 hours. Ridge now counts the missing hour as
  light sleep when your heart rate through it stays at your sleeping level, and the Stages
  card says so. If your heart rate says you were awake, or there is none, the night stays as
  the strap recorded it.

No server update needed.
