# Missing Workouts Update

## What's new

- **Workouts the strap detected reach the app again.** Since the clock change on 25 October
  the app stopped at the last workout recorded in summer time and never asked for the ones
  after it, so Fitness showed no new strap workouts however hard you trained. The strap kept
  them all: the first sync after this update brings them in, and your load, strain and
  calories for those days are recounted. Only the ones from the last day are announced. If
  you raised the detection sensitivity because of this, Standard is enough again (#4).
- **The strap's settings can be changed, and the page says how.** On the Strap page the
  settings are now grouped as Heart rate, Sleep, Stress, Blood oxygen and Workouts, each with
  a line on what it does. A switch turns something on or off, a row of buttons picks one of a
  few values (the low heart-rate and low SpO₂ alerts, detection sensitivity), and a row with
  arrows opens a list (how often heart rate is read, the high heart-rate alert). Every value
  offered is one the strap itself accepts; a change is written to the strap and read back,
  so the page shows what the strap holds.
  Ridge's sleep, recovery and workout numbers are built on continuous heart-rate readings,
  so reading less often leaves gaps. The strap's own heart-rate zones are no longer listed:
  Ridge never used them, it works with yours from your maximum heart rate (#5).
