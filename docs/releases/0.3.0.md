## What's new

- **Charge replaces "readiness now"** on the Recovery screen. Charge is a body battery from
  0 to 100, worked out minute by minute: sleep fills it (more in deep sleep, with a calm
  heart and a good HRV night); activity, stress and the waking day drain it. The card shows
  where you are now, where you were when you woke, the day as a curve with sleep shaded, and
  what recharged and drained it. Stress only drains it once it stays over a threshold
  learned from your own calm readings. Readiness now could only be lowered by exercise, so a
  stressful day at a desk left it untouched. Recovery, the morning score, is unchanged.
- **The strap's own PAI** on Activity: the 7-day total against the goal of 100, today's
  points and the week's days.
- **Breathing disturbances** on Sleep: how often your blood oxygen dipped during the night,
  per hour the strap measured, next to your usual. It is the strap's own count, not a
  sleep-apnea test.
- **HRV through the day**: the strap also measures HRV as SDNN 3–4 times a day. "HRV
  through the day" on the Recovery screen opens its chart.
- **More data from the strap** (since 0.2.6): HRV as SDNN, PAI and the nightly breathing
  summary are now synced. The strap keeps only about a week of breathing summaries, so sync
  at least weekly; background sync does this for you.

Every formula and constant behind Charge is written down in `docs/spec/02-science.md`
(§2.12). No study validates a body-battery formula; the model follows OpenStrap's.

**Update your server too.** Charge and the new cards come from the server. With an older
server the app still works, and those cards say the server needs updating. No database
change this time. To update (deploy/README.md, "Updating"):

```sh
cd ridge-helio-strap && git pull && cd deploy && docker compose up -d --build
```
