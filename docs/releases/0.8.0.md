# Your Sessions Update

## What's new

- **Add a session the strap missed.** On Fitness, tap **Add session** next to the day: pick the
  sport, when you started and when you finished. Ridge shows the strap's heart rate through
  it, its strain, load and heart-rate zones, just as for a workout the strap recorded, and the
  day's load, Energy and calories count it. A workout the strap detected inside it is shown as
  your session, not twice (#4).
- **Fix a workout the strap got wrong.** Open any workout and tap **Edit** to change its sport
  or stretch it to when you really started and stopped (a two-hour climb the strap saw as ten
  minutes, say). The strap's own record stays as it was; Ridge shows and counts your version,
  and **Undo edit** brings the strap's back.
- **Hide a workout that wasn't one.** **Hide** takes a false detection off your lists and out of
  your load. It stays listed under the day as hidden, with **Show** to bring it back. A session
  you added is deleted instead.
- **Calories for sessions without the strap's figure.** The minutes of a session you added or
  re-timed now count from your movement like any other minute, where before a workout without
  calories added nothing. Movement misses effort without steps (cycling, climbing, lifting),
  so the day's total says so.
- **Turn workout detection on or off, and set how sensitive it is.** On the Strap page,
  **Detect workouts** and **Detection sensitivity** (High, Standard, Low) now change the
  strap's own settings, which before only the Zepp app could. Each change is read back from
  the strap, so the page shows what the strap holds. Higher sensitivity notices more
  workouts, and more that weren't. The Helio has one detection category, its general
  "Activity", so detection is on or off rather than per sport (#4).

Your sessions and edits are backed up with the journal and come back with a restore.

**Server update needed** if you back up to a server: older servers refuse the new sessions
upload, and the phone keeps them waiting until the server is updated.
