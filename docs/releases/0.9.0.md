# Health Connect Update

## What's new

- **Your days in Health Connect.** Turn it on under Settings → Integrations → Health Connect
  and Ridge writes there what it works out, for the other apps on your phone that you allow
  to read it:
  - each day's steps, distance, total and active calories and basal rate;
  - your heart rate, minute by minute as the strap read it;
  - each night's sleep with its stages (naps too), resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen and
    breathing rate;
  - your workouts as Fitness lists them, with your edits and added sessions and without the
    hidden ones;
  - your VO₂max estimate.

  The last 30 days are written when you turn it on, then each day again after every sync, so
  a late sync or an edit corrects what was written rather than adding to it.
- **Write-only, and yours to undo.** Ridge asks Health Connect only for permission to write
  and reads nothing from it. Turning it off keeps what was written or removes it, as you
  choose.
- **If your phone counts steps too**, Health Connect holds both counts. Which one other apps
  see is set in Health Connect itself, under Data sources and priority.
