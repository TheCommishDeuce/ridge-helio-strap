## What's new

- **Mark a day as Active, Sick, Injured or On a break.** A chip under Today's week strip opens
  the choice; the day keeps it. A marked day shows its icon on the calendar, in Fitness's week
  and training calendar, in the journal and in the day's summary.
- **Heart rate, stress and steps cards are simpler and match.** Each one has the day's figure
  with its change against your usual, two smaller figures beside it (peak and low, highest and
  lowest, distance and active minutes), the day as ten-minute bars and how the day split.
  Steps now show the steps in each ten minutes, not a running total.

**Update your server too.** The server backs up the day's status with the rest of the
journal. Until it is updated it refuses the status, and journal entries made after it wait on
the phone. No database change. To update (deploy/README.md, "Updating"):

```sh
cd ridge-helio-strap && git pull && cd deploy && docker compose up -d --build
```
