# Detected Workouts Update

## What's new

- **"Workout detected" after a sync.** When the strap recognises a workout by itself, the
  next sync tells you, as the Zepp app does: the sport, how long, when it started. Tap it to
  open the workout, where you can fix its sport or times, or hide it if it wasn't one. Only
  workouts that ended within the last day are announced, and each only once. No connection is
  held for it, so the strap's battery is unaffected. A workout inside a session you already
  logged in openGym isn't announced. Turn the notices off under Android's notification
  settings for Ridge ("Detected workouts") (#4).
- **One activity, one row.** A strap workout during an openGym session or a Dawarich walk or
  ride is now shown as that session or ride, not as a second entry beside it: on Fitness's day,
  the strain list, Today's timeline (openGym sessions) and the training calendar (openGym
  sessions). The ride or session lists the strap's workouts inside it under **On the strap**,
  each still with Edit and Hide, and a workout's page shows what it's part of. Your load,
  strain and calories are unchanged: they still count from what the strap measured.
- **The training calendar counts openGym sessions.** A month of gym sessions used to show as
  "0 workouts".
