## What's new

- **A new look.** Every card is built the same way: the metric's icon in its colour, the
  value with how it compares with your usual, one chart, a few figures underneath. Each
  screen fades in from its own colour; the gauges are rings; Sleep sits on a night palette.
- **Today** shows last night's HRV, resting heart rate, breathing rate, SpO₂, time asleep and
  skin temperature against your usual, each with a slider. Heart rate, stress and steps have
  cards in the style of a stress card: three figures and a ring, the day coloured by heart-rate
  zone, stress level or steps adding up, and how the day split. Quick log sits under Your day.
- **Changes read as +6, −2 or ±0 vs usual**, green when better or about usual, yellow when
  worse, grey where neither is better; trends know which way is better too.
- **A full-day timeline** (Today → Full day): the day hour by hour, rings you pin and reorder,
  and a + on any hour to log a coffee, tea, a drink or your weight at that hour.
- **A calendar** in Today's and Sleep's bar: a month of rings for strain, recovery, the strap's
  sleep score or stress; tap a day to show it.
- **Sleep** opens any of the last seven nights, with the strap's score in the ring, the night
  in words, the four sleep-health checks as tiles and heart rate, HRV, breathing and SpO₂
  through the night.
- **Recovery** puts each part as a bar with its weight, then Charge and an illness check that
  shows last night's breathing rate and skin temperature against the 14 nights before.
- **Fitness** (was Activity): training load as your 7-day load against its range (below range,
  in range or a load spike), the strap's PAI, the week's steps, charts you pin, and a training
  calendar. New **Strain** and **Workout** screens: a workout's strain, heart rate, zones,
  cardio focus and the load it added.
- **Biology** (new tab): biological age (docs/spec/02-science.md §2.11) with its range, the
  fitness and sleep biomarkers that move it, and your other biomarkers' course. The Journal
  moves to Today's top bar, with quick log and your weight over time.
- **Fixes.** An illness flag now says which night raised it (a flag stays active for two days)
  and what last night found; a breathing rate under its baseline no longer reads "+-0.2".

No server change and no database change: just update the app.
