# Travel Update

## What's new

- **Steps stay right when you travel.** The strap starts its daily step counter again at its
  own midnight, and Ridge never set the strap's clock. After a flight to another timezone, the
  counter could start again before your midnight and the day showed almost no steps, on
  Today, in the week's steps and in the Health Connect export. Ridge now sets the strap's time
  and timezone to the phone's whenever it connects, as the Zepp app does. Days already affected
  are worked out again on the first start, and Health Connect gets the last 30 days rewritten.
- **Workouts the strap missed are offered.** The strap's own detection can miss a long, steady
  effort such as a hike. Fitness now lists a long stretch of raised heart rate, or of steady
  walking at a raised heart rate, as "possible workout, not recorded". **Add** opens the session
  sheet on its window so you can pick the sport. Until you add it, nothing is counted.
- **Rides on Dawarich tracks are told by heart rate.** A stretch of a track faster than walking
  is a ride when your heart rate over it reaches zone 2, and being carried (tram, metro, car)
  when it does not. Before, it depended on whether the strap had recorded a workout. Time on a
  track is now time moving: stops are no longer counted.
- **Dawarich tracks a few minutes apart are one outing.** When Dawarich cuts a walk or a ride
  into pieces at a pause, Ridge joins them into one row again.
- Today no longer says "load load spike".
