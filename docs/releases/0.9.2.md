# Fitness Polish Update

## What's new

- **Pick a session's times on your heart rate.** Adding a session or editing a workout now
  shows your heart rate around the session, with its window marked. Press and drag to find
  where it started or ended, then tap **Set start** or **Set end**. **Whole day** shows the
  full day, and the time pickers are still there for exact times.
- **Sessions you added show their figures.** A session you added, or a workout whose times
  you changed, now shows its average heart rate and an estimate of its calories on the list
  and on its page, marked "est.". The estimate comes from your heart rate (Keytel et al.
  2005) and needs your profile and a weight. It is shown only: the day's calories still
  count those minutes from your movement.
- **Back goes back to the day you were on.** Opening a workout from an earlier day on
  Fitness and going back now returns to that day instead of today. Tap Fitness again to go
  to today. A "Workout detected" notice opens on the workout's day too.
- **Hidden workouts fold away.** The workouts you hid on a day are now one "N hidden" line
  that opens to show them, with **Show** on each. Short walks fold the same way.
